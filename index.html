Essential Lisbon Tips

🚌
Get a Viva Viagem card — load it with credit for trams, metro, buses, and ferries. Far cheaper than single tickets.
🍷
Eat like a local — look for "prato do dia" (daily special) at local tascas. Full meal with wine for €10–12.
🌅
Miradouros at sunset — Portas do Sol, Santa Catarina, and São Pedro de Alcântara are stunning. Go 30 min before sunset.
👟
Wear comfortable shoes — Lisbon's hills and cobblestones (calçada portuguesa) are beautiful but unforgiving on flats.
💳
Cash for small spots — many tascas and market stalls are cash-only. Keep €20–30 on hand daily.
☀️
Late June heat — sun is intense by midday. Plan outdoor sightseeing before noon or after 5 PM.

🗓️ Suggested 8-Day Itinerary

Day 1 · May 30
Arrival & Baixa/Chiado

Check in, freshen up, then stroll Rua Augusta. Grab a pastel de nata at Pastéis de Belém (there's a central location too). Evening: rooftop drinks at TOPO Chiado.

Day 2 · May 31
Alfama & Fado Night

Morning: São Jorge Castle, wander Alfama's alleys. Afternoon: Portas do Sol miradouro. Evening: authentic fado dinner at Tasca do Chico (book ahead!).

Day 3 · Jun 1
Belém District

Jerónimos Monastery, Torre de Belém, MAAT museum. Have the famous original pastel de nata. Take tram 15E or Uber — don't battle tram 28 with a crowd.

Day 4 · Jun 2
Day Trip: Sintra

Take the train from Rossio (40 min, €2.35). Pena Palace first thing — queues build fast. Also Quinta da Regaleira if time allows. Back by 6 PM.

Day 5 · Jun 3
LX Factory & Santos

Sunday market at LX Factory (if applicable). Explore the trendy Santos & Cais do Sodré areas. Pink Street (Rua Nova do Carvalho) for evening drinks.

Day 6 · Jun 4
Day Trip: Setúbal & Arrábida

Rent a car or join a tour for Arrábida Natural Park — turquoise waters rivalling the Caribbean. Pack a picnic. Stop at a winery on the way back.

Day 7 · Jun 5
Bairro Alto & Príncipe Real

Morning: Príncipe Real's antique market and botanical garden. Lunch: tasca lunch. Afternoon: MUDE or Museu do Azulejo. Evening: sunset at Miradouro da Graça.

Day 8 · Jun 6
Last Bites & Departure

Time Out Market for a last Lisbon feast. Pick up ginjinha, tinned fish, and azulejo tiles as gifts. Head to Humberto Delgado Airport — allow 45 min from centre.
