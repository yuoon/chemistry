Essential Tips
🚌
Viva Viagem Card
Load it with credit — works on metro, trams, buses & ferries. Far cheaper than single tickets.
🍷
Prato do Dia
Daily special at local tascas — full meal with wine for €10–12. Best value in Europe.
🌅
Miradouros at Sunset
Portas do Sol, Santa Catarina, São Pedro de Alcântara. Arrive 30 min before sunset.
👟
Comfortable Shoes
Lisbon's hills and cobblestones are beautiful but brutal. No new shoes on this trip.
💳
Keep Cash On Hand
Many tascas and market stalls are cash-only. Keep €20–30 daily.
☀️
Beat the Midday Heat
Sun is intense by noon. Plan outdoor sightseeing before 11 AM or after 5 PM.

8-Day Itinerary
Day 1
May 30
Arrival & Baixa/Chiado

Check in, stroll Rua Augusta. Pastel de nata stop. Evening rooftop drinks at TOPO Chiado.

Day 2
May 31
Alfama & Fado Night

São Jorge Castle, Alfama alleys, Portas do Sol. Evening: authentic fado at Tasca do Chico — book ahead!

Day 3
Jun 1
Belém District

Jerónimos Monastery, Torre de Belém, MAAT. The original pastel de nata at Pastéis de Belém.

Day 4
Jun 2
Day Trip: Sintra

Train from Rossio (40 min, €2.35). Pena Palace first thing. Quinta da Regaleira if time allows.

Day 5
Jun 3
LX Factory & Santos

LX Factory market. Explore Santos & Cais do Sodré. Pink Street for evening drinks.

Day 6
Jun 4
Day Trip: Arrábida

Rent a car — turquoise waters rivalling the Caribbean. Pack a picnic. Stop at a winery on the way back.

Day 7
Jun 5
Príncipe Real & Graça

Antique market, botanical garden, Museu do Azulejo. Sunset at Miradouro da Graça.

Day 8
Jun 6
Last Bites & Departure

Time Out Market feast. Pick up ginjinha, tinned fish, azulejo tiles. Airport: allow 45 min from centre.
